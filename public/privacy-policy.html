Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2025

CourseLynx values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, .edu email, school, and profile info.
  • Usage Data: Chats joined, messages, activity analytics.
  • Device Data: IP address, device type, cookies, app logs.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide and improve CourseLynx services.
  • To verify academic eligibility and prevent fraud.
  • To deliver relevant ads and personalized features.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Service Providers (cloud hosting, analytics, communications).
  • Advertisers (only anonymized or aggregated data).
  • Legal Authorities (when required by law or valid legal process).

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may:

  • Access a copy of your data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising.
  • File a complaint with a data protection authority.

5. Data Retention

  • Data is retained as long as you maintain an account.
  • Certain records may be retained for legal or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Security Measures

  • CourseLynx uses encryption, access controls, and monitoring.
  • However, no system is completely secure. You use CourseLynx at your own risk.

7. International Transfers

If you use CourseLynx outside the U.S., your data may be transferred internationally.

8. Children's Privacy

  • CourseLynx is not intended for children under 16 years old.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Contact

Questions? Contact us at contact@courselynx.com.
